## Caveats (read first)

* **In-sample.** All of v3, v4 and v4.1 were designed after seeing the afternoon results; improvements there are expected by construction.
* **Tiny sample.** A few bets on ~15 matches; ROI, Brier, BSS and LL skill are noise-dominated.
* **Min-odds fill.** Every bet assumed filled at its minimum odds with the first-tier quarter-Kelly stake; no bookmaker odds were seen (reverse-pricing mode everywhere, so v4.1's lift gate applies to every line).
* **Gaps.** No afternoon states in 13:45–14:19, 14:23–14:35, 14:35–16:46, 16:54–17:10 UTC (box paused).
* **FotMob** = only what was logged live; no shots-inside-box in the states, so the chasing-corner SIB exception never fires (1.03 cap always applies).
* **Prior-only benchmark** = same engine and state (score, corners, clock, stoppage), time-decayed pre-match lambdas, no multipliers, no shrinkage; each rule set is scored on its own chosen lines. For v4.1 the lift gate *selects* lines where model and prior disagree by ≥ 6 %, so BSS/LL skill there measure exactly that bet: whether the in-play adjustment beat the prior.
* **net_multiplier** = λ_model_remaining / λ_prior_remaining per family (goals: home+away; corners: total μ incl. Gamma-Poisson shrinkage of the observed corner pace and the chasing cap). Unders allowed in blowouts (|diff| ≥ 3 blocks Overs only).
